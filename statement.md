# Problem Statement
College students often lose track of their daily minor expenses, leading to depleted monthly allowances before the month ends.

# Scope of the Project
A console-based Python application designed to log daily expenses and monitor budget health in real-time.

# Target Users
University students living in hostels or PGs who need a simple way to manage their pocket money.

# High-Level Features
- Initial budget configuration
- Categorized expense logging
- Real-time balance analytics
- Automated low-balance warning alerts
